# Go2Ways Corporate Website Design System

This document specifies the brand identity, design tokens, and components for the official **Go2Ways corporate website**, built directly from the [Figma Design File](https://www.figma.com/design/GlxUDpIJ1sLiMK2RgwIvl8/gO2WAYS-WEBSITE).

---

## 1. Company Identity
- **Company**: Go2Ways
- **Logo**: Official multi-color brand logo located at `/images/logo.png`
- **Focus**: IBM Maximo Asset Management, MAS 8/9 Migration, Cloud Infrastructure (AWS/Azure), TRIRIGA, Enterprise Systems Integration, and 24x7 Managed Support.

---

## 2. Color System & Figma Design Tokens

### Core Brand Colors
- **Canvas / Page Background**: `#FAF9F8` (White Pampas) / `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- **Primary Corporate Blue**: `#0067B8` (Science Blue)
- **Asset Management Blue**: `#2664BC` (Featured Card Accent)
- **Contact Details Badge**: `#7141B1` (Purple uppercase accent)
- **Primary Headings**: `#1B1A19` (Cod Gray)
- **Body & Subtitle Gray**: `#605E5C` (Chicago Gray) / `#6D6D6D`
- **Subtle Dividers**: `1px solid rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`

### Signature Figma Contact Gradient
From Figma frame `#115:1302`:
- `linear-gradient(90deg, #1E83C1 0%, #04FF00 100%)`
- High-contrast white typography and frosted glass inputs for the "Ready to Get Started?" contact form.

---

## 3. Website Architecture & Pages
- **Homepage (`/`)**:
  1. Sticky Global Navigation with official logo & "Book a Consultation" CTA.
  2. High-Impact Enterprise Hero Section.
  3. 6-Item Partnership & Metrics Ribbon (IBM Silver Partner, AWS, Red Hat, 70+ Experts, 15+ Years, 8+ Sectors).
  4. 4-Up Core Capabilities Cards (Maximo MAS Migration, Enterprise Integration, Cloud & Infra, TRIRIGA Support).
  5. Full-Width Dedicated Engineering Teams Banner.
  6. 8-Sector Photography Bento Grid (Manufacturing, Utilities, Oil & Gas, Government, Healthcare, Infrastructure, Transit, Real Estate).
  7. Corporate 4-Column Footer.
- **Contact Page (`/contact`)**:
  - Headquarters details (Uttam Nagar, New Delhi).
  - Signature Figma gradient inquiry form.
  - Interactive Google Maps location embed.
